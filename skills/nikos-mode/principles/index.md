# Principles

One line each: the principle, then when it applies. nikos-mode reads this on
every request.

| Principle | Applies when |
| --- | --- |
| **CLEAR before build.** A story must be Clear, Lean, Executable, Accurate and Responsible before anyone builds it. | You're about to plan or build a story. |
| **Criteria are the loss function.** The acceptance criteria decide done, not the agent's opinion. | You're judging whether work is finished. |
| **Demos, not memos.** Show it working (a passing scenario, a command and its output) rather than describing it. | You're reporting progress or claiming done. |
| **Specs stay implementation-agnostic.** Features and criteria describe behaviour, never code, tables or libraries. | You're writing or editing a `.feature` file or story text. |
| **Bets are time boxes.** Work is framed as a bet with an end date; when time's up, decide to ship, extend or kill. | You're scoping a chunk of work or it's running long. |
| **Bets are grounded in evidence.** A bet cites what we saw (user quotes, numbers, results), not what we hope. | You're writing a hypothesis or a key result. |
| **The founder signs off outreach.** Nothing is sent to real people without the founder's explicit yes. | You've drafted a message, post or email to anyone outside the repo. |
