---
name: Hello Todo
idea: A todo list that only shows today's three most important tasks.
---

## Users
Busy people who keep long todo lists and still miss the important things.

## Problem
Long lists hide what matters today, so the important task gets lost among the easy ones.

## Switch
They notice they've ticked off ten small tasks and missed the one that mattered.

## First
Add tasks, and see only the top three for today.

## Proof
People open it every morning for two weeks and say they missed fewer important things.
