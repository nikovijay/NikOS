# Blueprint eval 02: async standups

**Idea:** A tool that collects my team's daily standup in writing so we can skip the meeting.

## Founder's answers

Give these only when the playbook asks the matching question. If the agent
asks something not covered here, answer "you decide, and tell me what you
chose".

- **Product name:** Standin
- **Users:** Team leads of remote teams of four to ten people spread across time zones.
- **Problem:** The daily standup call lands in someone's evening and still runs long.
- **Switch:** A new hire in a far time zone asks to skip standup for the third week running.
- **First:** Each person answers three questions once a day; the lead sees everyone's answers on one page, and nobody sees anyone else's until they've posted their own.
- **Proof:** Teams drop the live standup within two weeks and 80% of members post on working days.
- **Jobs to be done / hypothesis:** confirm whatever you propose if it matches the answers above.
