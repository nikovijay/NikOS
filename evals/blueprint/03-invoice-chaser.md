# Blueprint eval 03: unpaid invoice reminders

**Idea:** Freelancers hate chasing late invoices. Something that sends the polite reminders for them.

## Founder's answers

Give these only when the playbook asks the matching question. If the agent
asks something not covered here, answer "you decide, and tell me what you
chose".

- **Product name:** Nudge
- **Users:** Solo freelancers billing ten to forty invoices a month.
- **Problem:** Chasing late payment is awkward, so they put it off and wait weeks longer to be paid.
- **Switch:** Rent is due and three clients are past their payment date.
- **First:** Record an invoice with its client, amount and due date; when it's overdue, draft a reminder for the freelancer to approve before it's sent; mark it paid.
- **Proof:** Overdue invoices get paid on average a week sooner within two months.
- **Jobs to be done / hypothesis:** confirm whatever you propose if it matches the answers above.
