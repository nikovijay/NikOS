---
name: Standin
idea: A tool that collects a remote team's daily standup in writing so the team can skip the meeting.
---

## Users
Team leads of remote teams of four to ten people spread across time zones.

## Problem
The daily standup call lands in someone's evening and still runs long.

## Switch
A new hire in a far time zone asks to skip standup for the third week running.

## First
Each person answers three questions once a day; the lead sees everyone's answers on one page, and nobody sees anyone else's until they've posted their own.

## Proof
Teams drop the live standup within two weeks and 80% of members post on working days.

## Jobs to be done
When I lead a remote team spread across time zones, I want to collect each person's daily standup in writing, so I can skip the live call without losing track of what everyone is doing.

## Founding hypothesis
We believe team leads of remote teams of four to ten people will ask their teams to post standups in writing instead of joining the live call because the call lands in someone's evening and still runs long. We'll know we're right when teams drop the live standup within two weeks and 80% of members post on working days.
