---
name: Nudge
idea: A tool that tracks freelancers' unpaid invoices and drafts polite payment reminders for them to approve.
---

## Users
Solo freelancers billing ten to forty invoices a month.

## Problem
Chasing late payment is awkward, so they put it off and wait weeks longer to be paid.

## Switch
Rent is due and three clients are past their payment date.

## First
Record an invoice with its client, amount and due date; when it's overdue, draft a reminder for the freelancer to approve before it's sent; mark it paid.

## Proof
Overdue invoices get paid on average a week sooner within two months.

## Jobs to be done
When I have invoices past their due date and bills to pay, I want to chase each late client without the awkwardness, so I get paid sooner and stop putting it off.

## Founding hypothesis
We believe solo freelancers billing ten to forty invoices a month will send the reminders Nudge drafts for them to approve because chasing late payment is awkward, so they put it off. We'll know we're right when overdue invoices get paid on average a week sooner within two months.
