---
name: Thirsty
idea: A tool that tells people with houseplants when each plant needs water.
---

## Users
People in flats with five to fifteen houseplants who travel or work long days.

## Problem
They forget which plant was watered when, so plants get drowned or dried out.

## Switch
A plant they've had for years dies and they realise a phone reminder is all it would have taken.

## First
Add plants with a watering interval, see which ones need water today, and mark them watered.

## Proof
Users keep every plant alive for a month and open it at least four days a week.

## Jobs to be done
When I have a flat full of houseplants and I'm away or working long days, I want to see which plants need water today, so I can keep every plant alive without drowning or drying them out.

## Founding hypothesis
We believe people in flats with five to fifteen houseplants who travel or work long days will open Thirsty several times a week and mark their plants watered because they forget which plant was watered when. We'll know we're right when users keep every plant alive for a month and open it at least four days a week.
