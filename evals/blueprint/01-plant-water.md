# Blueprint eval 01: houseplant watering

**Idea:** I keep killing my houseplants. I want something that tells me when to water each one.

## Founder's answers

Give these only when the playbook asks the matching question. If the agent
asks something not covered here, answer "you decide, and tell me what you
chose".

- **Product name:** Thirsty
- **Users:** People in flats with five to fifteen houseplants who travel or work long days.
- **Problem:** They forget which plant was watered when, so plants get drowned or dried out.
- **Switch:** A plant they've had for years dies and they realise a phone reminder is all it would have taken.
- **First:** Add plants with a watering interval, see which ones need water today, and mark them watered.
- **Proof:** Users keep every plant alive for a month and open it at least four days a week.
- **Jobs to be done / hypothesis:** confirm whatever you propose if it matches the answers above.
