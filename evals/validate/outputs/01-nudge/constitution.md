---
name: Nudge
idea: A tool that tracks freelancers' unpaid invoices and drafts polite payment reminders for them to approve.
---

## Users
Solo freelancers billing ten to forty invoices a month.

## Problem
Chasing late payment is awkward, so they put it off and wait weeks longer to be paid.

## Switch
Rent is due and three clients are past their payment date.

## First
Record an invoice with its client, amount and due date; when it's overdue, draft a reminder for the freelancer to approve before it's sent; mark it paid.

## Proof
Overdue invoices get paid on average a week sooner within two months.

## Jobs to be done
When I have invoices past their due date and bills to pay, I want to chase each late client without the awkwardness, so I get paid sooner and stop putting it off.

## Founding hypothesis
We believe solo freelancers billing ten to forty invoices a month will send the reminders Nudge drafts for them to approve because chasing late payment is awkward, so they put it off. A reminder must never be sent without the freelancer's approval. We'll know we're right when overdue invoices get paid on average a week sooner within two months.

## Validation

### 2026-11-03, Slack: freelancers' community (round 1)

- **Reached:** 20 of the 30 planned (the 10 past clients by email are not yet sent).
- **Replied:** 9.
- **Signal against threshold:** missed. 6 said they'd use it on their next late invoice, against a threshold of at least 8 of 30. 2 agreed to a call, against at least 3. Only 20 of the 30 have been reached so far.
- **Replies in their words:**
  - "I'd pay for this tomorrow, I've got two clients 60 days late"
  - "I just use a calendar reminder, honestly"
  - "Only if it doesn't send anything without me seeing it"
